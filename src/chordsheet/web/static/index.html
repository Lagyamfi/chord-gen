Song view: each section once. Section names are guesses: click a name to fix it (applies to its repeats, saved for this song). Click a chip in the form to loop that section, a line's letter to jump to it. Space: play/pause · ← →: previous/next bar · click a bar to jump · drag across bars (or shift-click) to set a loop · L: loop on/off · Esc: clear loop. Purple chords are outside the key: the ones worth learning. With a capo, the big chord is the shape to play; "sounds" is the chord you hear.
